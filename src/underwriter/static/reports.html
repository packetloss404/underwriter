How to read this report.

The counting rules behind the headline.

Decision cycles. A cycle is a distinct ID present in decision records. The cycle count includes exploratory records; operational impact counts only live-strategy rejected records.

Recorded days. Days with decision records in America/New_York. This is a data-coverage measure, not a trading calendar or a measure of uptime.

Operational classification. Known scan, preflight, veto and backend failure codes, plus the historical “Model unavailable” detail marker. Read the original records to investigate a category.

Time comparisons. The immediately preceding window has the same number of calendar days. Holidays and missing observations are not normalised; coverage is shown beside counts.

Historical context. A period containing old failures is not a claim that the service is failing now. The overview carries the current operating picture.

Complete counts, legible charts. Charts use daily, weekly or monthly buckets for long selections. Aggregation preserves every selected record and leaves missing money measurements blank.
